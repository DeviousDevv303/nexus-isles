# Observer (build order step 6 — journal-derived reporting)

`report.ts` derives displayed world counts from the journal (Annex A.1). `dynastyScore.ts` implements the Annex B `dynasty-score-v1` projection. It requires a valid hash chain and a successful world replay before scoring. The observer journals the score version, weights, axis definitions, active-lineage snapshot, tick convention, and unresolved interpretations at epoch open. At close it journals only the boundary and source journal head; score emissions are always recomputed from the verified journal prefix, never stored as snapshots.

The score normalizes each available raw axis against the active lineages at epoch close and applies `sqrt(normalized)`. Missing weight is held in abeyance, never redistributed. Each lineage reports `coverage`, `observedWeightedAverage`, `weightedSubtotal`, and `missingAxes`; these metrics are not a cross-coverage ranking. Available zero evidence remains a known zero; absent evidence is `MISSING` (`rawValue: null`). Territory-years is owner-reserved and has no definition in this contract. Verified artifacts stay `MISSING` without third-party citation/use events. Successor continuity stays `MISSING` until the owner resolves the heir and own-agency clock reading. Wealth-resilience stays `MISSING` without income-source, upkeep, and productive-deployment records; its subweights are explicitly PROVISIONAL. These axes are never inferred from proposals, predictions, free-text console entries, inventory, or resident position.

Survival counts full resolved ticks after the open boundary. A resident is counted as alive at a tick's start if their birth was before that tick and their death was at or after it. A birth during a tick begins its first full own-agency survival tick on the following tick; a death during a tick counts as alive at that tick's start. This convention is stored in the epoch-open record.

`World.openDynastyScoreEpoch` and `World.closeDynastyScoreEpoch` add audit-only entries. World restore accepts those entries after a committed tick and replays them without changing game state. Tests for the score contract are in `tests/at21/dynasty-score.test.ts`.
