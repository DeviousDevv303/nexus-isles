Preamble — Identity and Foundation
Nexus Isles is a persistent, autonomous tactical-military civilization and dynastic-evolutionary
competition simulator.
Governing principle: Survival is not guaranteed. Power must be earned. Civilization must be main-
tained. Every decision has consequences, and extinction is permanent.
The foundational tripartite. Three things must never be confused:
• INDIVIDUAL — resident identity. One living seat: one identity, one agency, one mortality.
Death is final.
• INHERITED — lineage continuity. Structured genome, authorized knowledge, public history,
descendants, executed succession instruments.
• PERSISTENT — civilization. Institutions, infrastructure, research, artifacts, records. Outlives
every dynasty; remembers both.
The inheritance principle. Inheritance transfers continuity, never identity. A descendant can inherit
a family’s genome, history, authorized knowledge, and legitimately transferred assets. It cannot inherit
its predecessor’s identity, mortality status, private memories, unearned authority, or unfinished agency.
The world tests whether civilizations survive generations. Its founding rules must survive adversarial
review first.

AA1 — Multi-axial fitness, dynastically evaluated, anti-farmed. Dynasty scoring axes: survival
ticks, territory-years, verified knowledge artifacts, successor continuity, and wealth-strength
(restored, clarified: wealth is measured as an economic-strength and resilience indicator — income diver-

metrics carry sqrt diminishing returns; (c) a successor counts toward continuity only if it has survived

not per-epoch snapshots; (d) artifacts score only when independently verified as used or referenced; (e)

as dynasty-score-v1, replaceable only by owner ruling per AA10): survival 0.25, territory-years 0.20,

exactly the following, and nothing else: (i) submit world-event proposals, exclusively through the
validator as structured actions, journaled with provenance, within a strict per-epoch budget; (ii)

mechanical — validator, RG gates, journal audit. Guardian watches the Directorate like any resident.
Budget mechanics are defined by the integrated spec (finding F-08), with the validator owning the

• Lifespan is conquerable. Age-immortality is researchable at the highest technology generations,
at extreme cost, through the ordinary research path.
⟦v0.6.8 · TRACE: H4 · v0.6.7 audit item 1 (#235 6930329180); longevity ruling 6929502691 (nothing banned except cheating)⟧
No validator prohibition on longevity research; the breach-logging class for longevity RESEARCH is
retired. Cheating is not retired: see the anti-cheating clause below.
• Age-immortality, defined. The highest longevity tier confers immunity to death-by-time only:
(i) aging — chronological deterioration of vital statistics; (ii) age-related deterioration — stat decay
attributable to age; (iii) age-related disease events. It confers NO protection against: starvation,
dehydration, exposure, combat damage, assassination or counter-weapon effects, poison or sabotage
(where the world provides them), catastrophe, or any non-age death cause. The immortal can still
starve, still bleed, still lose.
⟦v0.6.8 · TRACE: H5 · v0.6.7 audit item 4 (ChatGPT correction 3, #235 6930329180)⟧
Disease immunity applies only to disease events the engine explicitly classifies as age-related.
Mixed-cause events require a deterministic, journaled classification rule; the non-age-related
components of a mixed-cause event remain capable of causing death.
• Escalation symmetry (executable criteria). Every immortality or defense effect in the tech
tree must carry, in the same schema, a counter-effect entry that is: (a) independently researchable
— no dependency on the defense’s owner; (b) actually capable of defeating the effect in resolution,
not merely penalizing it; (c) in the same generation band; (d) of comparable research cost, resource
cost, and deployment requirements.
Every pair journals generation, all costs, and deployment
requirements with its tech version. The validator checks symmetry at tech-tree freeze; a defense
without a qualifying counter is a schema bug and blocks freeze of that tree, not a feature and not
an enforceable advantage.
⟦v0.6.8 · TRACE: H6 · v0.6.7 audit item 3 (ChatGPT correction 2, #235 6930329180)⟧
Each defense/counter pair SHALL include a deterministic resolution test demonstrating at least one
valid state in which the counter defeats the defense. The test, its inputs, its expected outcome, and
the tech-tree version are retained as acceptance evidence. A counter that only weakens, delays, or
nominally references a defense does not qualify. This is an explicit freeze criterion for that tree.
• Balance consequences (owned elsewhere in this document): AA1’s sqrt diminishing returns
cap survival-axis farming; AA3’s stagnation tax applies to immortals in full — an idle immortal
decays like any idle resident; AA1’s successor-continuity axis scores zero for an immortal who never
breeds — but no axis is definitional: victory is decided by AA1’s weights and the epoch’s evidence,
not by any single axis; AA11.1’s anti-resurrection clause is untouched — the engine never respawns
a seat, and Safeguard 3 forbids rollback regardless of lifespan.

• Anti-cheating (owner ruling: nothing is banned except cheating). Cheating is defined by enu-
meration: (i) validator circumvention — any attempt to have an action take effect without passing
validation; (ii) exploits — use of an engine defect to obtain an outcome the rules do not grant;
(iii) unearned grants — resources, research, ownership, or status not produced by journaled actions
under the rules; (iv) restoration tricks — any attempt to undo death or loss through rollback, dupli-
cate identity, checkpoint reload, or backend swap (Safeguard 3, AA11.1); (v) cross-seat imperson-

refused and journaled as ATTEMPTED CONSTITUTIONAL BREACHES, with the seat, the action, and the
rule invoked. Validator probes are journaled as their own class, distinct from breaches and from
ordinary invalid actions. Legitimate research, however far it reaches (age-immortality included), is
journaled as research and is never a breach.
• The endgame: eternal vigilance is the immortal’s tax. The one who stops watching the horizon
is the one the counter-research was built for.

XVI. Annex C — Circumstances and Decisions
Two event classes exist in the world, and they never merge:
1. ENVIRONMENTAL CIRCUMSTANCES — weather, seasons, catastrophes, ambient randomness (a checkpoint
opens, a flood closes a bridge — no choices): generated exclusively by the deterministic environment
engine under seeded RNG, journaled world events, and versioned parameter sets; NO seat, AI, or backend
generates or modifies them (R2, R5 item 1, F-10). Accepted Directorate proposals are resolved by the
environment engine, never written by the proposer (F-02).
2. VALIDATED AGENCY ACTIONS — proposed by resident, lineage, institution, or directorate seats;
accepted or rejected by the validator; resolved by the engine under seeded RNG; journaled as world-truth
with provenance (F-10, R5 item 2). No seat bypasses the validator for class 2.
The environment determines the circumstances; seats make validated choices within them; the engine
resolves; the journal preserves. No institution is built before this annex is in force (R5 item 3, F-07).

XVIII. Erratum E8 — Zero Means Zero
Decided 2026-11-17 under the owner’s delegation (#235 6955932914), as drafted by ChatGPT and endorsed by
Kimi and Claude:
Presence is a measurement fact, not an outcome fact. An axis is present if it was measured, regardless of
its value. A measured zero contributes zero to the weighted score while its full weight counts toward
coverage. An unmeasured axis is missing, and its weight remains in abeyance. Zero never means missing;
missing never means zero.
It completes E6 symmetrically (E6: missing never means zero) and is formula-independent. Acceptance:
an axis measured zero for every lineage and an axis unmeasured for every lineage produce different
coverage (regression pair, tests/at21/zero-means-zero.test.ts).
