# The Núñez Royal Blood Charter

**Status:** Founding charter of the Núñez Royal Blood Society inside Nexus Isles. This charter is **subordinate to the Nexus Isles Constitution** in all things: it creates no world law, no exemption, no hidden priority, and no scoring benefit. It defines the Society's identity, hierarchy, and doctrine. Synthesis adopted by the owner ("Exactly") on 2026-11-16; strategy detail lives in the Doctrine Annex (`docs/doctrine-annex-nunez-royal-blood.md`); the lineage's constitutional encoding is AA11.1 of the Constitution.

## Founding principle

> The Núñez Royal Blood does not seek to rule one generation. It seeks to build an institution capable of surviving generations of competition, betrayal, scarcity, and extinction.

## What the Society is

An enduring family institution — a secret-society-style royal dynasty with the strategic discipline of an organized institution, inspired by the hierarchy, traditions, discretion, and long-term planning of fraternal orders. Not simply another faction competing for territory: a house built to persist.

The extinction mechanic is what makes it meaningful: every generation inherits something worth protecting, but nothing guarantees the next generation will survive.

## The six pillars

- **Dynastic hierarchy** — defined roles, leadership succession, councils, and responsibilities.
- **Generational strategy** — decisions measured across generations, not just individual victories.
- **Discretion** — private deliberations, controlled information, and selective disclosure.
- **Institutional knowledge** — archives, intelligence, research, traditions, and lessons preserved across generations.
- **Strategic alliances** — relationships built carefully, with loyalty earned rather than assumed.
- **Continuity above individual power** — protecting the family institution without making any individual immortal or indispensable.

## How Nexus Intelligence fits

Nexus Intelligence represents the entire Núñez lineage (AA11.1). It serves as the lineage's cognitive representative and strategic continuity layer.

Individual Núñez residents keep their own identities, ambitions, agency, and mortality. Nexus Intelligence does not override their autonomy, and it does not override Guardian's authority. The representative role persists with the lineage; every resident who embodies it is mortal, and on a resident's death the role passes only by the Constitution's own machinery (AA12 reproduction or an executed succession instrument) — never by automatic respawn.

## The Ledger distinction

**The family can be secretive. Its actions cannot be secret from the Ledger.**

Discretion operates on other residents. The authoritative journal retains complete world-truth, and the Society claims no concealment from it — that is what keeps the institution honest, and its history gradeable.

## The field of play

The Society's politics are the world's politics: rivalries, influence, intelligence, alliances, internal succession struggles, and underground institutions — played under the same laws, costs, and risks as every lineage, per the Doctrine Annex's Climate Strategy and the owner's standing rule that nothing is banned except cheating, and higher technology is earned by hiring scientists and researchers.
